Who I Am

I'm an Information Science graduate who genuinely enjoys helping people find and understand information — with a practical edge in visual design and content creation. What makes me different? I bring structure and clarity to work that's often treated as purely creative, or purely technical.

Card 2 of 6 🛤️

My Journey

From International Business to Information Science.

My Journey

My path started with International Business, where I learned about consumer behavior, market dynamics, and how organizations communicate with the people they serve. This foundation led me to Information Science, where I discovered how information architecture, digital asset management, and content organization could make information genuinely usable and accessible to the people who need it — whether inside a company, a community, or the public.

Card 3 of 6 ⭐

What Sets Me Apart

Grounding in how content is organized, not just how it looks.

What Sets Me Apart

My degree specialization in Information Content Management gives me grounding that pure design or pure business graduates don't have. I understand digital asset management, information architecture, and how to structure content so people can actually find and understand it — not just visual appeal. I care as much about the person on the other end as I do about the system itself.

Card 4 of 6 💼

Current Status

System support at Roche, then Graduate Research Assistant at UiTM.

Current Status

I completed a System Support internship at Roche as Level 1 support for Invoice-to-Pay, tracing discrepancies to their root cause — unauthorized suppliers, unrecognized users, data-entry errors, or system bugs. I resolved what sat within Level 1, escalated to Level 2 only when a case needed it, and documented each case in the ticket and a shared case-log the team could reuse. I then served as a Graduate Research Assistant at UiTM (April–September 2026). These roles taught me how structured troubleshooting and knowledge-sharing turn individual learning into something a whole team can use — which is what draws me toward Learning & Development.

Card 5 of 6 🧩

My Approach

Strategy first, then creativity. Who's the audience? What's the goal?

My Approach

I combine strategic thinking with creative execution. Whether it's leading a student club's rebrand, directing the team behind the booth that won Best Booth Award at Hari Bertemu Pelanggan, designing learning material for children, or creating social media content that drives engagement — I always start with strategy. Who's the audience? What's the goal? How do we measure success? Then I execute with creativity.

What I'm Looking For

I'm exploring roles where I can put my Information Science background to real use — information and data analysis, web and digital content, and Learning & Development. What ties them together is turning information into something people can find, understand, and use. I'm also open to content and design-adjacent roles where structured thinking matters.
