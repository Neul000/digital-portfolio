About Me

The story behind the information science graduate

Who I Am:

I'm an Information Science graduate who genuinely enjoys helping people find and understand information — with a practical edge in visual design and content creation. What makes me different? I bring structure and clarity to work that's often treated as purely creative, or purely technical.

My Journey:

My path started with International Business, where I learned about consumer behavior, market dynamics, and how organizations communicate with the people they serve. This foundation led me to Information Science, where I discovered how information architecture, digital asset management, and content organization could make information genuinely usable and accessible to the people who need it — whether inside a company, a community, or the public.

What Sets Me Apart:

My degree specialization in Information Content Management gives me grounding that pure design or pure business graduates don't have. I understand digital asset management, information architecture, and how to structure content so people can actually find and understand it — not just visual appeal. I care as much about the person on the other end as I do about the system itself.

Current Status:

I completed a System Support internship at Roche (MNC environment), where I learned how visual communication drives clarity in professional settings — translating complex technical processes into clear documentation, creating visual guides, and collaborating with cross-functional teams across APAC. I went on to serve as a Graduate Research Assistant at UiTM (April–September 2026), applying structured documentation and research methodology skills. Together, these experiences taught me how to thrive in structured, deadline-driven environments while bringing creative problem-solving to operational challenges.

My Approach:

I combine strategic thinking with creative execution. Whether it's rebranding a student club (which won us Best Booth Award), designing educational courseware for children, or creating social media content that drives engagement — I always start with strategy. Who's the audience? What's the goal? How do we measure success? Then I execute with creativity.

What I'm Looking For:

I'm exploring opportunities where I can put my Information Science background to real use — roles where I get to help people find, understand, and engage with information, whether that's through content, information literacy, community or student engagement, or user-facing information services. I'm also open to content and design-adjacent roles where structured thinking matters. Still figuring out the exact fit, but drawn to work where I get to guide and connect people with information, not just organize it behind the scenes.

Open now for full-time roles in Content Creation, Design, or Digital Marketing.
